
## C rebuttal and vote

Atlas Desk wins on immediate task legibility, but loses to Living Atlas as a distinctive whole: cobalt/chalk could read like another GIS product. Living Atlas gives this scientific tool a more authored cultural home and visibly departs from the previous dark-planet treatment. The navigable-exhibit proposal offers the largest first impression, but Earth must not become a game lobby that hides the question, evidence or backend state. Its navigation overhead and visual effects also risk repeating the original latency complaint.

Vote: **Living Atlas**, provided the product definition stays readable in front of Earth, lighting embeds the sphere in the pale surface, and the evidence/scenario/local-AI pages receive the same layout craft as the hero. Keep the single reversible globe; reject cinematic entry gates, decorative telemetry and automatic scenario inference.
