# Earth texture assets

The Earth colour, cloud, and specular textures are taken from the public Three.js planet examples:

- https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg
- https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_clouds_1024.png
- https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_specular_2048.jpg

Source project and license: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js and https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE

These textures are illustrative background imagery. They do not represent live observations, measured heat, or an analysis result.
