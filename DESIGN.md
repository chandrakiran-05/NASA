# Living Atlas — design system

The owner delegated research, adversarial critique and final selection to three design agents. Full context: docs/AGENT-DESIGN-BRIEF.md. Research and debate: docs/design-research/.

## Decision

Living Atlas won: an editorial Earth-system field atlas, not another dark sci-fi globe landing page. Source inspirations were principles from Awwwards Lusion/Cyera/Waabi profiles and live sites, Siteinspire Whole Earth Index/Stripe Press, and usable science/product interfaces Earth Nullschool, Felt and Linear. No source code, distinctive art, slogans or proprietary imagery copied. Access and visual-inspection limits are documented per researcher.

## Palette and type

Mineral paper #eeeae1, dark botanical ink #27342e, supporting ink #626c60, star blue #086bdc for observed-world highlights and sage #68765c. Newsreader regular/italic is the display voice; DM Sans handles body and controls. Native monospace is reserved for actual commands/schemas. The real Earth supplies color. Geographic linework belongs to the Earth, not a generic background grid.

DigiTwin turns paper to charcoal-claret #25232b with peach #e9a17f and warm ivory. Color is reinforced by explicit simulated labels. The same scene rolls 180 degrees; it never remounts. Its headline reads "Another Earth - a future to explore."

## Composition

Fine-rule masthead, NASA mark and quiet navigation. Oversized single Earth word behind a naturally lit centered sphere; italic front-readable subline in the surrounding negative space. A physical orbital line gives depth without fake telemetry. Compact Apple-inspired frosted glass prompt and round blue action. A larger north-up Earth crosses the fold into a reserved continuation space, preserving one canvas. Useful question index below the short editorial process spread; no equal feature-card grid.

Evidence is a case sheet: record index, wide plot, finding margin, server-returned statistics, source and export. Twin is a scenario sheet with visible data/assumptions/results and honest capability state. AirLLM is a local field guide with an original process diagram and copyable setup steps.

## Motion and trust

Maintain existing single renderer, DPR cap, reduced-motion support and zero settled-paused/offscreen drawing. No blocking intro, cursor replacement, full-page animated blur or extra postprocessing. Pointer lighting is restrained. Space toggles automatic Earth rotation while its accessible globe is focused. AirLLM illustrates data flow and layer loading; motion pauses offscreen, in hidden tabs, with its pause control, and for reduced-motion users. Histories remain observations with units/date/provenance; scenario outputs always say simulated with disclaimer. Backend contracts unchanged.
